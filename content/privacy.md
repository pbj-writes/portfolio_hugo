This page describes the privacy practices of the **Fitbit → Strava Weight Sync** app, a personal automation that reads body weight from the Google Health API and writes it to a Strava athlete profile.

## Who uses it

The app is operated by the owner of this site for their own account only. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.

## What data it accesses

The app requests read-only access to Google Health body measurement data. It uses only the most recent body weight measurement.

## How data is used and stored

The current weight value and the time it was recorded are written to the Strava athlete profile and published on the [Fitbit → Strava Sync project page](/portfolio/strava-weight/) of this site. No other health data is read, stored, shared, or sold. Access credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never published.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner at bot@pbjwrites.info.
